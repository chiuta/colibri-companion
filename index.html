Your own AI, on your own computer.

Colibri Companion is the friendly face of colibri: it finds the right model for your computer, downloads it in one click and lets you chat. No account. No cloud. No command line.

Ready in three simple steps

No technical knowledge needed. If you can use a web browser, you can use this.

Download and open

Pick your system below, unzip the file and double-click Colibri Companion.

Choose a model

The app checks your computer and marks the model that fits it. One click to download — pause or resume whenever you like.

Start chatting

When the download ends, the chat opens by itself. Ask anything — everything stays on your computer.

Watch and learn

Seven short videos with subtitles in 8 languages. Press the CC button to choose yours.

Recorded in the built-in demo mode, so no real model was needed.
